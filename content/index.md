# A Sappy Goodbye, From The Creator

I like sappy goodbyes. I dunno, I have a thing for them.

Anyway, we're here now! The final term, and the final test! Maybe you've already finished the test by the time you read this.

In any case, this whole series has been a joy to make. I started this out because when I was in secondary school, my CCA president (great guy, one of the smartest people of around my age I know) did the something similar, made some notes and gave them to the whole cohort. I just felt like that was a good idea and tried to do it too.

The first one was on Python, I think. It was a Jupyter notebook that everyone had to download and open in their browser. It was awful.

Then I discovered Obsidian, the software that we used to write the very thing you're reading right now, and soon got Jabriel on board. And, again, we're now here.

I just wanna say thank you, for reading up to this point. For reading what we put out.

And, as well, thank you for being here. ITE is by far the most fun I've had in any academic environment. Even if I haven't interacted with you much, you've made this class what it is. I can't thank you enough for that.

Goodbyes scare me, that's the truth. But, I guess it's scarier not being able to say goodbye anyway.

If you've liked what we put out, or if it's helped you in any way, maybe send a message to me or Jeb. We'd love to hear it.

I told you it'd get sappy, right?

Otherwise, I guess this is goodbye. I hope you pursue what you like, and keep loving it.

Best wishes, and until next time!
